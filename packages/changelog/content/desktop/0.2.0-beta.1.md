## Dictation

- **Any app with focus.** Dictation now inserts into whatever has keyboard focus when transcription finishes, including Electron apps such as VS Code, Cursor, Slack, Discord, Notion and Obsidian, browsers, and terminals such as Terminal, iTerm2, Ghostty and Warp. It works with any keyboard layout, including Dvorak, AZERTY and Cyrillic layouts. Password fields and Secure Keyboard Entry still block it.
- **Sounds.** A short sound plays when dictation starts and when it stops. Turn them off with **Dictation sounds** in **Settings → Dictation**. They stay silent while Librelog records a meeting.
- **During meetings.** Dictation and chat voice input now work while Librelog records a meeting, for example to type into the call's chat. The meeting keeps recording, and what you dictate is part of its audio and transcript.

## Menu bar

- Closing the window keeps Librelog running in the menu bar, without a Dock icon or an entry in the app switcher, so dictation and meeting detection keep working. Open it again from the menu bar icon, Spotlight, or Applications. With **Show tray icon** off, the Dock icon stays. To quit, choose **Quit Completely** from the menu bar icon or press ⌘Q twice.

## Known limitations

- Librelog is not notarized by Apple yet. The first launch needs **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- After an update, macOS may ask again for microphone, screen and system audio recording, accessibility, or keychain access. Dictation into other apps needs accessibility access.
- Librelog needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
