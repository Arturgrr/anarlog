## Annotate your screen (experimental)

- **Annotate** records the display under the pointer while you draw on it in red and talk. When you finish, Librelog writes numbered key frames with your marks, a contact sheet that shows them side by side, and a summary of what you said at each frame.
- It is off by default. Turn it on in **Settings → Annotate** with **Enable Annotate**, then allow **Screen Recording** when macOS asks.
- Press Option + Shift + S to start, and again to finish. Hold the same shortcut to draw wherever the pointer moves; let go to stop drawing. Escape also stops drawing and never stops the recording.
- A small capsule shows the elapsed time, with a pen to draw by dragging, a stop button, and a discard button that asks first.
- Your narration is transcribed with the transcription provider you chose for dictation.

## Hand an annotation to a coding agent

- When an annotation is ready, its summary is copied to your clipboard.
- Press Option + Shift + A in the agent's input to paste the last annotation: first the contact sheet's file path, then the summary. In Claude Code and similar terminal agents, the path attaches the image.
- Agents connected through Librelog's MCP server can read annotations themselves with `list_annotations` and `get_annotation`.

## Where annotations are kept

- Each annotation is a folder under `annotations` in Librelog's data folder, with the screen recording, your narration, the key frames, the contact sheet and the summary. **Show annotations folder** in **Settings → Annotate** opens it.
- **Keep annotations for** decides how long they stay: **Week** (the default) deletes the video and audio after 7 days and the rest after 30, **Month** after 30 and 90 days, and **Forever** deletes nothing.
- A recording can show anything that was on your screen. Pasting an annotation into an agent sends its image and text to that agent's provider.

## Known limitations

- Annotate was written without a Mac to try it on. It has been built and tested by automated checks, but nobody has recorded with it yet. If something stays on screen or the pointer stops reaching other apps, press the Annotate shortcut again or click the capsule's pen, and tell us.
- Annotate records one display, for at most 20 minutes, and is available only in the macOS app downloaded from the website.
- Pasting the last annotation is made for terminal agents. In other apps the image's file path is pasted as text.
- Quitting Librelog while an annotation is being recorded or finished discards it.
- There is no menu bar item for Annotate yet; use the shortcuts.
- Librelog is not notarized by Apple yet. The first launch needs **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- Librelog needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
