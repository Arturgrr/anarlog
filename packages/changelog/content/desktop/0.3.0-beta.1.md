## Dictation

- **Dictate again while one transcribes.** You no longer wait for a dictation to appear before starting the next. Up to five dictations can wait to be transcribed, and they are inserted in the order you made them, each one wherever keyboard focus is when its text is ready. Text that is ready waits while you record the next dictation or a new shortcut, so it never arrives while you hold the keys.
- **Cancel just one.** Escape cancels only the dictation you are recording; the ones already waiting still finish. **Cancel dictation** in **Settings → Dictation** cancels all of them.

## Dictation history

- **Settings → Dictation history** lists your dictations, newest first, with when you dictated, the app the text went to, and the model that transcribed it. A dictation whose text could not be inserted is marked **Not inserted**, so you can still copy it. Search by text, app or model, copy or delete a dictation, or choose **Clear all**.
- Choose how long dictations are kept: 1 day, 7 days (the default), 30 days or forever. **Off** stops saving new ones.
- Turn on **Keep dictation audio** to also keep each recording, then choose **Transcribe again** with your transcription model or an on-device model. Up to five earlier versions are kept, and **Restore** brings one back. Audio is off by default.
- The history stays on your computer. It is not synced, exported, or available to the CLI and MCP server.

## Paste last dictation

- A shortcut pastes your newest dictation again into the focused field, including one you transcribed again. Change it or turn it off under **Paste last dictation shortcut** in **Settings → Dictation**.
- Turn on **Keep last dictation on the clipboard** to leave each dictation's text on the clipboard after it is inserted, or after the insertion fails.

## While dictating

- Choose what happens to other audio while you record a dictation under **While dictating** in **Settings → Dictation**: **Do nothing** (the default), **Mute**, **Lower audio** to a level you pick, or **Pause media**. Librelog undoes the change when recording ends, and the stop sound plays once your sound is back. Dictations that follow each other closely keep it quiet in between.
- If Librelog quits unexpectedly while your sound is muted or lowered, it restores it the next time it starts, unless you changed it meanwhile.
- Nothing changes while Librelog records a meeting, so you never silence a call.
- Your computer does not go to sleep from inactivity while you record a dictation.

## macOS

- The paste last dictation shortcut is Option + Shift + V, and **Paste last dictation** is also in the menu bar icon's menu.
- **Pause media** pauses Music, Spotify and VLC, but only those you allow Librelog to control. Choosing **Pause media**, or clicking **Allow…** under **Media player access**, asks macOS for each one that is running and shows which are allowed. A dictation never asks.
- **Also pause the browser** pauses a browser tab that is playing sound, and plays it again only if the pause worked. It needs the System Audio Recording permission.

## Windows and Linux

- The paste last dictation shortcut is Control + Alt + Shift + V on Windows and Control + Alt + V on Linux.
- **Pause media** pauses apps and browser tabs that report what they play to Windows' media controls, or players that support MPRIS on Linux.

## Known limitations

- Librelog is not notarized by Apple yet. The first launch needs **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- After an update, macOS may ask again for microphone, screen and system audio recording, accessibility, or keychain access. Dictation into other apps needs accessibility access.
- Librelog needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
- On macOS, **Pause media** reaches only Music, Spotify and VLC. macOS can ask for each one only while it is running, so open a player before clicking **Allow…**; a player you haven't allowed keeps playing. To change your answer, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation**.
- **Mute** and **Lower audio** act on the default output device only.
