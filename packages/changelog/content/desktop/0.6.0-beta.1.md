## Dictation in Insights

- **Insights** now has a **Dictation** section: how many dictations you saved, how many **Words**, your **Words per minute**, your **Day streak**, **Days with dictations**, and your five **Most-used apps**.
- The figures come from **Dictation history**, so they cover only the dictations Librelog still keeps. With history kept for 7 days, a streak can't be longer than about a week; keep history longer in **Settings → Dictation history** to see more.
- Words per minute is all your words divided by all your recording time, including the pauses while you hold the shortcut, so it reads a little lower than your speaking pace.

## Badges are gone

- **Insights** no longer has collectible badges. The rest of the page, including the yearly activity and the weekly streak, is unchanged.

## Choose where the dictation indicator shows

- **Settings → Dictation → Dictation indicator** lets you choose **Top**, **Bottom**, **Hidden** or **Notch**. **Top** is the default, as before.
- **Bottom** puts the indicator above the Dock, with the live text above it.
- **Hidden** shows nothing while you dictate. The start and stop sounds, if on, are the only cue.
- **Notch**, on a MacBook with a notch, makes the indicator grow out of the notch: an orange microphone and level bars while you speak, a spinner while Librelog transcribes, then a green check mark. On a display without a notch it sits at the top.
- If the indicator can't show for any reason, dictation now carries on without it instead of stopping.

## Known limitations

- The **Notch** indicator was written without a Mac to try it on. It has been built by automated checks, but nobody has seen it yet: if it looks off, sits beside the notch, or leaves anything on screen, switch back to **Top** and tell us.
- **Bottom** and **Hidden**, and the **Dictation** section of Insights, have been checked with automated tests, but not yet on a Mac by the project.
- Insights does not yet show how much Librelog changed your dictated text, because dictation does not rewrite text yet.
- Badges you collected before are no longer shown.
- Librelog is not notarized by Apple yet. The first launch needs **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- Librelog needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
