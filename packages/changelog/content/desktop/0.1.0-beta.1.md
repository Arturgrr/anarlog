<banner title="Welcome to the Librelog beta">
Librelog is a community fork of Anarlog that keeps your meetings on your Mac: no account, no subscription, no telemetry. This is a beta, so expect rough edges and tell us what breaks.
</banner>

## What you can do

- Take notes in meetings without a bot. Librelog records your microphone and system audio and transcribes them with an on-device model or a provider you set up with your own API key.
- Get summaries, titles, and chat from a local runtime such as Ollama or LM Studio, or from a provider with your own key.
- Keep notes, transcripts, and recordings in a local database and folder on your Mac.
- Let your agents read your meetings through the `librelog` CLI and local MCP server. Install them from **Settings → Developers**.

## Tell us how it goes

Choose **Help → Report Bug**, or open **Settings → General** and use **Report a problem** or **Send feedback**. Librelog opens a GitHub issue with your app version, macOS version, and chip filled in; nothing else is sent. **Show log files** opens the folder with recent logs if you want to attach them.

## Known limitations

- Librelog is not notarized by Apple yet. The first launch needs **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- After an update, macOS may ask again for microphone, screen and system audio recording, accessibility, or keychain access.
- On Intel Macs, on-device MLX transcription models are not available. Use a provider or a local transcription server instead.
