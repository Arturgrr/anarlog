# Licensing

Librelog, a fork of [Anarlog](https://github.com/fastrepl/anarlog), is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The MIT License covers every part of the repository except material that carries its own license.

Third-party material, such as bundled models, fonts, and adapted code, keeps its original license. [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) lists each item with its license and source. A license file next to a component takes precedence for that component.

## Contributions

Contributions are accepted under the MIT License. Before adding third-party code or assets, check that the license is compatible, keep the original copyright and license notices, and add an entry to `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` with the upstream source and revision.
