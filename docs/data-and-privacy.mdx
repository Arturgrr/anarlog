---
title: "Data, privacy, and retention"
description: "Understand what stays on your computer, what can leave it, and how long audio is kept."
---

Librelog stores its app data on your computer and captures meetings without adding a bot to the call. There is no Librelog account or server, and the app sends no usage analytics. Data leaves your computer only when you use a feature that needs it.

## What stays on your computer

Your notes, local transcripts, settings, recordings you choose to keep, and downloaded local models are stored locally. You can write notes, record audio, and use configured local models without sending meeting content anywhere.

Transcription and Intelligence API keys are stored in your operating system's secure credential store, not in the app database.

<Warning>
  Do not edit the app database directly — this can corrupt your notes. Use Librelog's export tools when you need a portable copy.
</Warning>

## What the app connects to

Librelog only contacts these destinations:

- The **Transcription** and **Intelligence** providers you select. A hosted Transcription provider receives the audio needed to create a transcript. A hosted Intelligence provider receives the text and instructions needed to create a summary or chat response.
- **Hugging Face**, to download on-device transcription models and Built-in language models you choose. No meeting content is sent.
- **GitHub Releases** for `Arturgrr/librelog`, to check for app updates. No meeting content is sent.

These features also send data, but only after you configure them:

- [Chat](/chat) sends the prompt and required context to your selected Intelligence provider.
- [Webhooks](/reference/webhooks) post meeting content, including transcripts, to the endpoints you add.

Built-in models run in a server Librelog starts on your Mac, reachable only from this computer with a key that changes on every start. Apple Reminders uses the accounts already on your Mac. Local providers, local Markdown exports, the CLI, and the local MCP server keep content on your computer.

Review a hosted provider's own retention and training policies before sending sensitive content. See [Models and providers](/models-and-providers) for how direct provider requests work.

## Keep a meeting fully local

1. Select an on-device model under **Settings → Transcription**.
2. Select Built-in models, LM Studio, Ollama, Unsloth, or eligible Apple Intelligence under **Settings → Intelligence**.
3. Avoid webhooks to remote endpoints for that content.

See [Use Librelog offline](/offline) for the full setup.

## Choose how long audio is kept

Open **Settings → Meetings** and find **Audio file retention** under **Audio**. Choose:

- **Don't save**
- **1 day**
- **3 days**
- **1 week**
- **1 month**
- **Forever**

<Warning>
  Once the retention period passes, the audio is gone for good — the meeting note and transcript remain, but the recording does not. If you need the recording later, right-click the meeting in the sidebar, choose **Show in Finder**, and copy the audio before its retention period ends.
</Warning>

[Separating speakers after a live on-device meeting](/models-and-providers#speakers-after-a-live-on-device-meeting) and learning new voiceprints both read the saved recording after the meeting, so **Don't save** turns them off. Both run on this computer.

Librelog uses bounded temporary audio while recording to recover gaps caused by interrupted transcription. Temporary audio is removed after recovery or cleanup; **Don't save** removes recording audio when capture stops. If cleanup fails, Librelog reports the failure and retries instead of claiming the audio was deleted. Once audio is gone, an incomplete transcript cannot be repaired from that audio.

<Warning>
  Recording-consent rules depend on where you and the other participants are located. Make sure you have permission before recording.
</Warning>
