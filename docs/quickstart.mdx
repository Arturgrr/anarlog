title: "Your first meeting"
description: "Set up Librelog and record a short test before your next call."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Librelog">
    Download the latest build from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Arturgrr/librelog/releases), open it, and follow the setup screen. See [Install the desktop app](/desktop-installation) for macOS builds and building from source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow audio access">
    Allow **Microphone** so Librelog can hear you. Allow **System Audio** so it can hear everyone else on the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your models">
    Open **Settings → Transcription** and pick an on-device model or a provider you configure with your own API key. Then open **Settings → Intelligence** and pick a local runtime such as Ollama or LM Studio, or your own provider. See [Transcription and AI](/ai-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record a short test">
    Open **Welcome to Librelog** and click **Record**. Talk for a minute, or play a video with someone speaking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    Stop listening. Open **Memos**, **Summary**, and **Transcript** to see what Librelog captured.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find your way around

- The sidebar keeps your recent notes within reach.
- The center of the app shows the open memo, summary, or transcript.
- **Chat** works with the open meeting and any additional context you add.
- **Settings** controls audio, AI providers, storage, integrations, and developer tools.

You can rename a note from its title and keep several notes open in tabs. A meeting note stays useful before, during, and after the call.

## Record a real meeting

Create a note, then click **Record** in the top-right corner. See [Record meetings](/meetings) for the full flow.

If Librelog cannot hear the call, start with [audio troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#audio-is-missing).
