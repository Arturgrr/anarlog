Librelog keeps people and organizations together with your meeting notes. Contacts are stored locally with the rest of your app data.

## Multiple email addresses

A contact can hold several email addresses. Open the contact and choose **Add email** under the Email field to add another address; each additional address can be edited or removed individually. The first email stays the primary address shown and copied elsewhere.

Librelog matches contacts by all of their email addresses — calendar attendees, session participants, and contact search all recognize every address on the contact. Merging duplicate contacts keeps both contacts' emails: the duplicate's address is preserved as an additional email.
