Librelog records your microphone and computer audio without joining the call as a bot. Each meeting stays together as an editable memo, transcript, and summary.

Librelog is local-first and open source. There is no account to create and no subscription: every feature is available to everyone. Transcription and summaries run on local models or on a provider you configure with your own API key.

## The short version

1. Create a note.
2. Click **Record**.
3. Take rough notes in **Memos** while Librelog listens.
4. Stop listening, then review **Summary** and **Transcript**.

Your app data is stored on your computer. Content leaves it only when you choose a hosted Transcription or Intelligence provider, or configure an integration such as a webhook. See [Data, privacy, and retention](/data-and-privacy) for the exact boundaries.
