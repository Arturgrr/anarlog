title: "Record meetings"
description: "Start, follow, and finish a meeting without adding a bot to the call."
---

## Start recording

Create a **New Note**, then click **Record**. This works for a call that is already open and for an in-person conversation.

To create the note and start recording in one step, press Command/Ctrl+Shift+N.

To start recording without leaving the app you are in, choose **Start a new meeting** from the Librelog icon in the menu bar or system tray. Librelog creates an untitled note and records it in the background, without opening its window. When you open Librelog, that note is open.

Librelog records your microphone and system audio. It does not join the meeting as a participant.

### Name the note first

Creating a note opens a small dialog with **Name** and **Type**. Enter a name and optionally a [note type](/notes#group-notes-by-type), then click **Create note**, or **Start recording** when you are starting a meeting. Leave the name empty and press Enter to continue; Librelog titles the note when it writes the summary.

You can rename the note from its title in the note header at any time, including while it is recording and after the meeting ends. The summary's title follows the new name.

Recordings you start from a Librelog notification or from **Start a new meeting** in the menu bar or system tray start right away, without the dialog. Name the note later from its title in the note header, set its type from **Meeting info** in the note's **•••** menu, or do both at once: right-click the note in the sidebar and choose **Rename and Set Type…**. An untitled note is titled when Librelog writes the summary.

<Warning>
  Make sure you have permission to record. Consent rules depend on where you and the other participants are located.
</Warning>

## During the meeting

Use **Memos** for your own notes. You can keep them rough: the generated summary uses the conversation and your memo as context.

Open **Transcript** to follow live text when your selected transcription model supports it. Models marked **After recording** process the audio when you stop.

If live transcription from a remote provider stalls during speech, Librelog attempts to reconnect while keeping audio capture running. The warning clears when finalized transcript text resumes.

### Floating bar

The floating bar is a small stop control that stays on screen while Librelog listens, so you can stop without opening the full window. When your model transcribes live, the bar can also expand to show the transcript. It is off by default. To turn it on, enable **Show floating bar** in **Settings → Meetings**, or choose **Show floating bar** from the **•••** menu of the note you are recording.

To turn it off during a meeting, click **×** on the bar or choose **Hide floating bar** from the same menu. Recording continues, and the bar stays off for later meetings until you turn it on again.

Without the bar, the Librelog icon in the menu bar or system tray animates while Librelog listens. Open Librelog and use **Stop listening** to finish.

## Finish the meeting

Click **Stop listening**. Librelog saves the recording according to your retention setting, finishes the transcript, and creates a summary when an Intelligence model is configured.

You can resume a finished meeting from the same top-right control if the conversation continues.

## Use an existing recording

Create or open an empty note, click the **•••** menu, then choose **Upload audio** or **Upload transcript**.

See [Import audio or a transcript](/import-recordings) for supported formats and the complete flow.

For automatic start, meeting detection, and stop controls, see [Automate meeting capture](/automatic-capture).
